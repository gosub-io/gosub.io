Welcome aboard! This is the very first post on the Gosub blog, on a fresh new 
website. A new place where we'll share progress reports, technical deep dives, 
and the occasional story from the depths of building a browser engine from scratch.

There is so much to talk about, and so many interesting things we face while 
building Gosub. Some of it is technical, some of it is design decisions, and some of 
it is just the story of how we got here. The blog gives us a place to share all of 
that.

Besides this blogpost, we have some other sources of information about the project.
For instance, we have [CodeMusings](https://codemusings.nl/tags/gosub) where we share short notes about the progress of 
the project as well. 

So that's it for our first entry. We just wanted to say hello and let you know that we're
here, and that we're excited to share our journey with you. Stay tuned for more 
updates, and if you want to get involved, check out our [contributing guide](/contribute/) 
or join the conversation in our [Zulip chat](https://chat.developer.gosub.io).
